Pro feature

The bibliography checker is part of Scholar Utility Belt Pro. A trial, purchase or App Pass unlocks it.

Reference list

Paste a numbered list, an APA-style list or BibTeX, or choose a .bib or .txt file. Nothing leaves this page unless you press the Crossref button below.

Result

This tool reports what the datasets shipped with the extension contain and nothing else. A venue that is not listed is not listed in those datasets; that says nothing about its quality.

Crossref lookup (optional)

Sends only the DOIs from this list to api.crossref.org, one request per second, up to 200 DOIs, to see whether each DOI exists and whether Crossref lists an update such as a retraction. Crossref sees your IP address.
