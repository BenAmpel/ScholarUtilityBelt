Pro feature

The librarian batch report is part of Scholar Utility Belt Pro. A trial, purchase or App Pass unlocks it.

Researchers

One researcher per line, up to 50. Add an institution after a bar (Name | institution) or an ORCID to narrow the match.

Read before you search: the names, institutions and ORCIDs you type are sent to api.openalex.org (with your OpenAlex API key if you set one in Options). One request per second, so 50 names take about a minute. Nothing is sent until you press the button, and Google Scholar is not used.

Confirm each person

Common names can match the wrong person. Pick the right candidate for each line, or leave it on "None of these". Only people you confirm go into the report. A suggestion is a guess, not a confirmation. A candidate is pre-selected only when its ORCID equals the one you typed.

Report

These numbers come from OpenAlex. They can differ from Google Scholar profiles, which count different sources and update on a different schedule. The h-index and citation counts here are OpenAlex's own. Works in the last 5 years counts the current year and the four before it, from OpenAlex's yearly counts. Top venues are the journals or repositories listed as the main location of the person's works.
