What's new in Scholar Utility Belt 0.6.2

This page opens once after the update and is stored inside the extension. It loads nothing from the network.

Changes in this version

  • Venue lists refresh on update. The bundled FT50, UTD24, ABDC, VHB, CORE, and CCF lists now update with the extension. Lists you edited in Options are left alone.
  • ACL Findings badge. Findings papers get their own badge instead of none.
  • CORE A/A* chip on author pages. The top stats row can count and filter CORE A and A* papers.
  • Settings gear on Scholar pages. The floating toolbar has a small gear that opens Options.
  • Badge matching fixes. Venues whose official title spells out the ordinal (NeurIPS and others) now match, EMNLP has its CORE alias, and a false ERA badge from a short entry is gone.
  • Options page. Toggle switches are back to their intended size.

More from Purplelink

Free web tools from the same studio that makes Scholar Utility Belt. These are plain links: the extension never contacts purplelink.llc, and nothing here is added to Google Scholar pages.

  • BibTeX Validator checks a .bib file for syntax errors, dead DOIs, and wrong authors or years.
  • Citation Generator turns a DOI, arXiv ID, or ISBN into APA, MLA, Chicago, or IEEE.
  • LaTeX to Word converts a .tex file or project .zip into a manuscript .docx.
  • Paper Review is a pre-submission review by four AI reviewers, from $9. References are checked free first.
